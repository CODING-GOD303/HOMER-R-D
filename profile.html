Our Story

How It
Started.

Homer Coffee Braga is present not only as a coffee shop, but also as a “second home” for everyone who visits. Established on February 14, 2026, Homer brings a warm, homey, and welcoming concept—a place where people can come, have conversations, enjoy music, and feel as comfortable as they do in their own home.

The name “Homer” is inspired by the philosophy of “home” and “lover” representing love, closeness, and comfort within a space. This concept was born from the founder’s experiences and long journey in building previous coffee businesses.

By prioritizing hospitality, warmth, and human connection, Homer aims to create an open space for everyone. Music, conversations, and a cozy atmosphere are essential elements that complete the experience. Homer believes that the beauty of a place comes from human interaction and the “beauty of imperfection.”
